## Summary Observations

- **Most-requested deliverable type:** Demo video (appears in the majority of requests)
- **Most active product areas:** MLz, Gravity/Infrastructure Management, AICI, UKO
- **Primary use contexts:** IBM-internal sales enablement sessions; on-site client workshops; client meetings
- **Key effort signal:** Design-heavy requests range from 5 to 60+ hours; many requests are ongoing or have open-ended scopes
- **Cross-cutting theme:** Content needs to be reusable across Seismic, sales plays, client meetings, and marketing pages — not built for single events
- **Gap visible in data:** Several entries lack estimated hours, deployment venues, or stated purpose — indicating requests are sometimes made before the full use case is defined
