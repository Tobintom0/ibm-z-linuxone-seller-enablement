# Leadership Presentation Brief
## IBM Z & LinuxONE Seller Enablement — Research Findings

**Prepared by:** Connections & Enablement Workstream
**Based on:** 13 interviews · 13 participants · EMEA, APAC, Americas
**Report version:** v1.6 · Living Research
**Presentation goal:** Secure leadership alignment on the systems gap and prioritise a response

---

## The One-Sentence Brief

> IBM Z and LinuxONE sellers are operating without a coherent enablement system — and it is costing deals.

---

## 1. What We Did

Qualitative research programme: 13 in-depth interviews (60–90 min each) with sellers and technical sellers across 6 countries. Participants included first-of-kind profiles: the first Horizon/white-space seller, the first Africa/MEA seller, and the first Americas technical seller ever included in this study.

## 2. The Central Problem: A Systems Gap, Not a Content Gap

Sellers have **three knowledge sources** — none designed for them, none connected to each other:

1. **Official IBM content** (Seismic/Sales Kit) — sellers stop returning after onboarding; content goes stale locally
2. **AI tools** (Claude, Copilot, Gemini, WatsonX) — independently adopted by 12 of 13 sellers as a workaround
3. **Experienced colleagues** — interrupted daily; tribal knowledge that disappears when people leave

The result: the most effective sellers succeed because of years of personal networks and self-built content. **None of it is captured anywhere official. When they move on, it's gone.**

---

## 3. The Two Collapse Points

Every stage of the sales cycle has workarounds. **Two stages have none:**

### Collapse Point 1 — Stage 1: No Prospect Intelligence
There is no IBM tool to identify which customers are running Oracle or VMware and approaching a server refresh — the primary entry-point trigger for LinuxONE sales.
> *"If there is a tool which can give me that insight as to what is the current stack of the customer... today if I'm targeting 5 opportunities, I'll be easily able to address 20."* — Karan Arya, India

### Collapse Point 2 — Stage 5: The POC Treadmill
Nearly every LinuxONE deal requires a full POC to prove Oracle database performance — a fact the market already accepts.
> *"We are proving that Oracle databases work well on LinuxONE — every single client. Same chemistry experiment. Why spend time? The world already knows."*
> *"Axis Bank POC: started June 2025, finished December 2025, got order March 2026. That's a 9-month engagement — but I have a 3-month window to close a deal."*

---

## 4. The Five Biggest Findings

### Finding 1 — AI has already replaced Seismic as the first stop for information (C5)
12 of 13 sellers independently built an AI workaround. This is not a behaviour quirk — it is a clear signal. If IBM does not build its own AI-assisted enablement experience, sellers will fully migrate to consumer AI tools.

### Finding 2 — Banking-only win stories are blocking deals in pharma and utilities (C5 · DEAL-BLOCKING)
Moesha Malik (Americas/Horizon) confirmed that ALL current win stories and use cases are financial/banking. Two separate clients — one utilities, one pharma — disengaged in the same week with the same reason: "We don't see how this fits for us because we are not in the banking industry." SMEs confirmed: **no pharma or utilities examples exist.**

### Finding 3 — The entire sales process is undocumented (C5)
Critical steps — ePricer approval process, Q2C, FOE submissions, DPL checks, credit checks, PO loading across 3 tiers — exist nowhere in writing. New sellers must interrupt experienced colleagues to discover each step. This is a daily cost to the organisation's most experienced people.
> *"There is no documentation for new sellers where to start. You need to find someone who knows it and ask... credit check, DPL, FOE — these steps are not documented anywhere."* — Bulent Palabiyik, Turkey

### Finding 4 — Seller-created content of institutional value is trapped outside the official kit (C3)
Matt Mondics (Americas) has built hundreds of self-built presentations in a personal OneDrive, including an IT optimisation demo that multiple sellers now use informally — it has **never entered Seismic**. Karan (India) independently built a Quantum Safety deck. Moesha (Americas) built a "What is LinuxONE" deck from scratch. There is no pathway for any of this to become official.

### Finding 5 — New markets are exposing gaps the kit was never built for (C2–C3)
- **Digital Assets / Haven (South Africa):** Jacqueline Coelho is competing against Thales with no competitive intelligence in the kit and no Africa-specific client references
- **Horizon / White-Space (Americas):** Moesha Malik's infrastructure-unaware clients have nothing in the kit that speaks to them
- **Enterprise Technical Briefings (Americas):** No customer-facing 'under the hood' technical brief exists — the format gap between the TLLB and the client overview is an active blocker for top-to-top enterprise conversations

---

## 5. The Structural Constraint That Content Cannot Fix

> *"We need 20 of him. How do we skill up people to be like him so that we can do more in parallel?"* — Matt Mondics, on Expert Labs implementation capacity

**Expert Labs has only 2 people nationally in the US who can implement LinuxONE. Both are booked through 2028 on an hourly basis.** Deals are stalling not because sellers can't sell — but because implementation capacity doesn't exist post-close. This is a talent pipeline problem that requires leadership action, not a content fix.

---

## 6. The Design North Star

> *"We made it easier for every seller to find the right content, understand what to say, and know what to do next — and help move deals forward."*

Three design problems. Three measurable outcomes:

| Problem | Design Response | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Can't find the right content | Unified hub + AI-powered search + update notifications | Return visits + time-to-find |
| Don't know what to do next | Seller onboarding process playbook | Time-to-first-deal for new hires |
| Can't connect features to CXO value | Capability-to-outcome navigator by industry + persona | Seller confidence surveys + deal stage progression |
