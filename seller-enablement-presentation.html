Five Biggest Findings
9 findings · Confirmed independently across regions
Finding 1

AI has already replaced Seismic as the first stop for information

12 of 13 sellers independently built an AI workaround — Claude, Copilot, Gemini NotebookLM, Watson Workshop. This is not a behaviour quirk. It is a clear signal: if IBM does not build its own AI-assisted enablement experience, sellers will fully migrate to consumer AI tools.

12 / 13 Participants · All Regions
Finding 2 · Deal-Blocking

Banking-only win stories are blocking deals in pharma and utilities

Moesha Malik (Horizon/Americas) confirmed ALL current win stories are financial/banking. Two clients — one utilities, one pharma — disengaged in the same week with the same reason: "We don't see how this fits for us because we are not in the banking industry."

2 Deals Lost · Same Week · Confirmed
Finding 3

The entire sales process is undocumented

Critical steps — ePricer approvals, Q2C, FOE submissions, DPL checks, credit checks, PO loading across 3 tiers — exist nowhere in writing. New sellers must interrupt experienced colleagues to discover each step. This is a daily cost to the most experienced people.

All Regions · Universal Finding
Finding 4

Seller-created content of institutional value is trapped outside the official kit

Matt Mondics (Americas) has hundreds of self-built presentations in a personal OneDrive — including an IT optimisation demo used informally by multiple sellers. Never entered Seismic. Karan built a Quantum Safety deck. Moesha built a "What is LinuxONE" deck. No pathway exists.

3 Regions · 3 Independent Instances
Finding 5

New markets are exposing gaps the kit was never built for

Digital Assets / Haven: Jacqueline Coelho is competing against Thales with no competitive intelligence and no Africa-specific references. Horizon / White-Space: Moesha's infrastructure-unaware clients have nothing in the kit. Enterprise Briefings: No customer-facing "under the hood" technical brief exists anywhere.
